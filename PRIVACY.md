# Privacy Policy for FoxHub

Last updated: October 2026

FoxHub is built to run completely locally inside your Firefox browser.

## 1. No Data Collection or Telemetry
FoxHub does not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data, browsing history, email content, or usage analytics. There are no telemetry scripts, no tracking pixels, and no external developer servers.

## 2. How Email Access Works
When you check your Gmail inbox through FoxHub, your browser connects directly to Google (`mail.google.com` and `googleapis.com`) using your existing Firefox session or your own local OAuth token. Email subjects, snippets, verification codes, and messages are processed strictly in your computer's memory and are never sent anywhere else.

## 3. How Sidebar Web Apps Work
When you open web apps like Instagram, WhatsApp, Discord, Spotify, YouTube, TikTok, X, Messenger, Telegram, or Reddit inside the FoxHub sidebar, those websites load directly from their official domains inside your browser using your normal Firefox cookies. FoxHub only adjusts layout styles and frame headers locally so those sites fit cleanly inside a narrow sidebar panel.

## 4. Local Storage Only
All of your settings, custom themes, uploaded background images, and snoozed email timers are saved locally on your device using Firefox's built-in `browser.storage.local` API. Uninstalling the extension or clearing extension storage removes this data immediately.
