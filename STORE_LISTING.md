## Name
FoxHub: Gmail, Social & Media Sidebar

## Short Summary (under 132 characters)
Opera GX-style sidebar web panels for Spotify (auto-pause), Instagram, Discord, WhatsApp, YouTube & 1-click Gmail 2FA codes.

## Full Description
100% Open-Source on GitHub: https://github.com/ShadiSairafi/foxhub-firefox-sidebar — zero developer servers, zero analytics, and zero tracking.

I built FoxHub because I miss Opera GX's sidebar web panels in Firefox and got tired of juggling ten pinned tabs just to check email verification codes, reply to messages, or pause Spotify while browsing.

FoxHub sits cleanly in your Firefox sidebar (or toolbar popup / popout window) so you can manage your Gmail inboxes and favorite web apps right next to whatever page you are browsing.

**What it does:**

* **Opera GX-style sidebar web panels:** Open **Spotify**, **WhatsApp**, **Discord**, **Instagram** (Feed, Reels & DMs), **YouTube**, **Telegram**, **TikTok**, **X**, **Messenger**, and **Reddit** in a dedicated Firefox sidebar—or pin any custom website URL.
* **Smart Auto-Pause & Auto-Resume for Spotify & YouTube:** Just like Opera's sidebar player, FoxHub automatically pauses your sidebar music whenever you play a video or audio in another Firefox tab and resumes right where it left off when you pause (configurable: *Pause & Resume*, *Pause Only*, or *Play Together*; ignores muted autoplay feed videos).
* **Background Audio Keep-Alive:** Keep Spotify (with gapless playlist transitions) and YouTube playing smoothly even when the sidebar is closed or Firefox is minimized, without losing your place when switching apps.
* **Grab Gmail 2FA codes without leaving your tab:** FoxHub automatically picks up all signed-in Firefox Gmail accounts (`u/0`, `u/1`, `u/2`, etc.), extracts 2FA codes and activation keys onto a one-click copy button, and lets you read threads, reply, archive, delete, star, snooze, or listen with text-to-speech.
* **Responsive mobile-friendly layouts:** Automatically adapts Discord's compact rail, Instagram modals/Reels, Spotify's player/volume bar, X, TikTok, and Reddit to fit narrow sidebars cleanly with an interactive video scrubber and quick bottom navigation bar.
* **Customizable shortcuts, Quick Mute & True Night OLED themes:** Configure global and per-app keyboard shortcuts, mute All/Email/Sites notifications with preset timers (`15m`, `1h`, `8h`, `24h`), and choose from True Night OLED (`#000`) + 8 warm themes, wallpapers, or custom uploads.

**Privacy & Security First:**
* **100% Open-Source & Local:** All code runs locally inside your browser—inspect every line anytime on GitHub (`ShadiSairafi/foxhub-firefox-sidebar`).
* **No telemetry or analytics:** FoxHub does not track clicks, collect usage stats, or talk to any external developer server.
* **Direct connections only:** Your browser connects directly to Gmail and the sites you open. All settings stay in your local Firefox storage.
