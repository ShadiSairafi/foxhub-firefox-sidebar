## Name
FoxHub: Gmail, Social & Media Sidebar

## Short Summary (under 132 characters)
Check multiple Gmail inboxes, copy login codes in one click, and use Instagram, Spotify, Discord, and more in your Firefox sidebar.

## Full Description
I built FoxHub because I got tired of juggling ten pinned tabs just to check email verification codes, reply to messages, or change a song while working.

FoxHub sits in your Firefox sidebar (and toolbar popup) so you can handle your emails and favorite web apps right next to whatever page you are currently browsing.

**What it does:**

* **Grab email codes without leaving your tab:** When a 2FA or verification code lands in your Gmail, FoxHub pulls the code onto a one-click copy button right on the email card. You can copy it from the sidebar and paste it straight into the login page next to it.
* **Multi-account Gmail inbox:** Automatically picks up all Gmail accounts signed into your Firefox session (`u/0`, `u/1`, `u/2`, etc.) in one unified view or separate account tabs. Read full threads, reply, archive, delete, star, snooze, or listen to emails with text-to-speech.
* **Built-in sidebar apps:** Open Instagram (including Reels and DMs), WhatsApp, Discord, Telegram, Spotify, YouTube, TikTok, X, Messenger, and Reddit right inside the sidebar, or add any custom URL you want.
* **Responsive mobile-friendly layouts:** Desktop websites usually look squished in a narrow sidebar. FoxHub automatically cleans up sidebars and overflow on sites like Instagram, Messenger, X, TikTok, and Spotify so they fit narrow widths cleanly, complete with a quick bottom navigation bar.
* **Keep-alive background tabs:** Switching from Spotify to Instagram or back to your email does not reload the page or cut off your music.
* **Warm themes & custom backgrounds:** Includes 8 warm color themes, illustrated backgrounds, custom wallpaper uploads, and soft acoustic notification chimes.

**Privacy & Security First:**
* **100% Local:** Everything runs inside your browser.
* **No telemetry or analytics:** FoxHub does not track clicks, collect usage stats, or talk to any external developer server.
* **Direct connections only:** Your browser only talks directly to Gmail and the sites you open. All settings stay in your local Firefox storage.
