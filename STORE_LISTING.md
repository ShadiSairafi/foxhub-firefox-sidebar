## Name
FoxHub: Gmail, Social & Media Sidebar

## Short Summary (under 132 characters)
Opera GX-style sidebar web panels for Spotify (auto-pause), Instagram, Discord, WhatsApp, YouTube & 1-click Gmail 2FA codes.

## Full Description
100% Open-Source on GitHub: https://github.com/ShadiSairafi/foxhub-firefox-sidebar — zero developer servers, zero analytics, and zero tracking.

I built FoxHub because I miss Opera GX's sidebar web panels in Firefox and got tired of juggling ten pinned tabs just to check email verification codes, reply to messages, or pause Spotify while browsing.

FoxHub sits cleanly in your Firefox sidebar (or toolbar popup / popout window) so you can manage your Gmail inboxes and favorite web apps right next to whatever page you are browsing.

**What it does:**

* **21 Opera GX-style sidebar web panels + custom apps:** Open **Spotify**, **WhatsApp**, **Discord**, **Instagram** (Feed, Reels & DMs), **YouTube**, **Telegram**, **TikTok**, **X**, **Messenger**, **Reddit**, **Twitch**, **ChatGPT**, **Google Gemini**, **Claude**, **Perplexity**, **SoundCloud**, **Apple Music**, **Bluesky**, **Notion**, **Google Calendar**, and **Google Keep** in a dedicated Firefox sidebar—or pin any custom website URL with per-app Mobile/Desktop User-Agent overrides and right-click *"Open in FoxHub Sidebar"*.
* **Mini "Now Playing" Bar, Volume Control & Smart Auto-Pause:** Control background **Spotify**, **YouTube**, **SoundCloud**, and **Apple Music** from anywhere with a top-edge movable seek scrubber, persistent per-app volume slider + mute/restore, and paused-only close button. Foreground sidebar apps (like Instagram Reels) automatically take priority over background music, and external Firefox tabs auto-pause & auto-resume right where you left off.
* **Background Audio Keep-Alive:** Keep Spotify (with gapless playlist transitions, forward-only timestamp continuity, and anti-throttling), YouTube, and SoundCloud playing smoothly even when the sidebar is closed or Firefox is minimized, without losing your place or volume when switching apps.
* **Quick Switcher (`Ctrl+K`) & 1-Click Gmail 2FA Codes:** Jump between apps or open any URL instantly with `Ctrl+K`, and automatically pick up all signed-in Firefox Gmail accounts (`u/0`, `u/1`, `u/2`, etc.) with 1-click 2FA code copying, quick reply, archive, delete, star, snooze, and text-to-speech.
* **Responsive mobile-friendly layouts:** Automatically adapts Discord's compact rail, Instagram profile reel modals & feed Reels, Spotify's player/carousels, X, TikTok, and Reddit to fit narrow sidebars cleanly with an interactive video scrubber and quick bottom navigation bar.
* **Searchable Settings, Backup/Restore, Quick Mute & True Night OLED:** Live-searchable settings, JSON export/import backup, optional Firefox `userChrome.css` presets, customizable global & per-app shortcuts, timed Quick Mute (`15m`, `1h`, `8h`, `24h`), and True Night OLED (`#000`) + 8 warm themes & custom wallpapers.

**Privacy & Security First:**
* **100% Open-Source & Local:** All code runs locally inside your browser—inspect every line anytime on GitHub (`ShadiSairafi/foxhub-firefox-sidebar`).
* **No telemetry or analytics:** FoxHub does not track clicks, collect usage stats, or talk to any external developer server.
* **Direct connections only:** Your browser connects directly to Gmail and the sites you open. All settings stay in your local Firefox storage.
