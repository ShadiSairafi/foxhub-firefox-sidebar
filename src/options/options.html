How Multi-Account Detection Works Locally

FoxHub automatically detects every Google account signed into Firefox (u/0, u/1, u/2, etc.) or connected via Local OAuth. Click "Sign in with Google Account" to log into another Gmail address in a popup window, and FoxHub will add it automatically as soon as you sign in.

Local-First Privacy Architecture

  • Zero External Servers: FoxHub has no backend server, no developer database, and no analytics or telemetry scripts.
  • Direct Browser-to-Service Connection: All Gmail, OAuth, and social web requests go directly from your Firefox browser to the official service (mail.google.com, googleapis.com, etc.).
  • First-Party Local Compose & Sent History: Emails sent from FoxHub are dispatched directly through your signed-in Gmail session and cached locally under your Sent filter.
  • 100% Local Storage: Your preferences, custom backgrounds, snoozed reminders, and saved video/scroll positions live exclusively in your local browser.storage.local container.
