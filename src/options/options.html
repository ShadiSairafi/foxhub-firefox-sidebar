How Multi-Account Detection Works Locally

FoxHub automatically detects every Google account signed into Firefox (u/0, u/1, u/2, etc.) or connected via Local OAuth. Click "Sign in with Google Account" to log into another Gmail address in a popup window, and FoxHub will add it automatically as soon as you sign in.

Local-First Privacy Architecture

  • Zero External Servers: FoxHub has no backend server, no developer database, and no analytics scripts.
  • Direct Browser-to-Gmail Connection: All requests go directly from your Firefox browser to mail.google.com or googleapis.com.
  • 100% Local Storage: Your preferences, custom backgrounds, and snoozed reminders live exclusively in your local browser.storage.local container.
